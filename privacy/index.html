Loot Table Works Privacy and measurement

A narrow measurement boundary

Aggregate counts, not player profiles.

Loot Table Works measures a small set of public funnel pages so we can tell whether people reach a useful tool, download the free kit, inspect a paid offer, or continue to a verified storefront. The generators and campaign data remain local to your browser.

What is counted

  • Aggregate views of eleven public funnel pages, including the checkout router, One-Shot Forge, and Campaign Workspace.
  • Allowlisted source, campaign, and content labels such as Pinterest, GitHub, a scheduled release campaign, or direct.
  • Fixed events for downloads, workflow handoffs, paid intent across seven paid offers, verified-store exits, and automatic checkout redirects.
  • A paid-intent, store-exit, or redirect event does not prove a purchase. Sales are verified only in the storefront payment record.
  • Coarse browser, system, country, language, and screen-width totals supplied by GoatCounter.

What is not collected

  • No cookies, advertising pixels, cross-site profiles, email addresses, account IDs, or user-entered names.
  • No campaign text, imported JSON or CSV, generated adventure, saved state, search term, seed, workspace ID, or exported file.
  • No raw query string or free-form campaign label is collected. Only fixed, allowlisted source, campaign, and content labels are counted; unknown values are discarded, and unknown referrers are reduced to a generic external label.
  • When checkout opens a storefront, only allowlisted source, medium, campaign, and content labels are forwarded. Unknown values are replaced with fixed offer defaults before the storefront URL is created.
  • No generator inputs, output contents, workspace interactions, form fields, local-save activity, or purchase details.
  • No individual-pageview storage in GoatCounter; that optional setting must remain disabled.

Control on this device

Do Not Track is honored, and common crawler or automated-browser signatures are excluded. You can also exclude this browser from Loot Table Works measurement. This preference is stored only in this browser.

Measurement provider

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-focused, open-source analytics service. It does not use cookies or persistent user identifiers. Its standard aggregate mode stores computed totals rather than raw IP addresses or user-agent strings.
