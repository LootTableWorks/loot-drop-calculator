# Gullwatch Aftermath Preview

Public owned-funnel preview for the private Gullwatch Aftermath continuation.

The page lets a visitor choose one of four prior endings, inspect its starting
pressure and clocks, share an attributed branch URL, and move into the complete
free Gullwatch one-shot or Campaign Workspace. It contains no private archive,
paid download, checkout, account requirement, background analytics, or
automatic external action.

The page also routes visitors to the owned Gullwatch Harbor campaign page,
where the complete four-session edition is described and free PDF and EPUB
samples are available. That route exposes no unverified checkout.

Canonical route:

`https://loottableworks.github.io/loot-drop-calculator/gullwatch-aftermath/`

Validation:

```powershell
node machines\machine-005\gullwatch-aftermath-preview\test\validate.js
```

Publication remains controlled by `release.json` and separate browser,
deployment, and public roundtrip QA.
